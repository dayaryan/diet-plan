Soya Keema Bhurji, Phulka & Iced Shikanji

CAL 384
PROTEIN 32.4g
FAT 6.2g
CARBS 49.9g
Ingredients (Dry Raw Weights):
• Soya Chunks (dry raw): 50g (~150g boiled)
• Whole Wheat / Jowar Atta: 40g (1 large phulka)
• Cold-Pressed Mustard/Olive Oil: 5g (1 metric tsp)
• Chopped Onion & Tomatoes: 60g onion + 50g tomato
• Crushed Ginger-Garlic & Green Chili: 10g
• Iced Shikanji (Replaces Chaas): Lemon + Jeera + Black Salt + Mint
Cooking Steps:
  1. De-bitter & Mince Soya: Boil 50g soya chunks in salted water 5 mins with 1 garlic clove. Rinse cold, squeeze hard with fists to remove bitter water. Pulse in blender for 2 seconds only for a fluffy scrambled keema texture.
  2. Sauté Masala: Heat 5g oil. Sauté 1/4 tsp jeera, crushed ginger, garlic, chili, and onions 4 mins until golden brown. Add chopped tomatoes, haldi, Kashmiri chili, coriander powder, and 2 tbsp water. Simmer 3 mins into a glossy paste.
  3. Toss Keema: Add pulsed soya keema and rock salt. Toss on high flame 3 mins. Sprinkle crushed kasuri methi, garam masala, and fresh lemon.
  4. Phulka & Drink: Knead 40g atta with warm water, roll out, puff over flame. Muddle lemon juice, black salt, roasted jeera, and mint into 300ml cold water for digestive Shikanji!

Velvety Thick Dal Tadka, Basmati & Kachumber

CAL 482
PROTEIN 22.2g
FAT 6.6g
CARBS 83.2g
Ingredients:
• Moong + Masoor Dal (dry raw): 70g (thick stew)
• Basmati Rice (dry raw): 45g (~125g cooked)
• Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil (Tadka): 5g (1 metric tsp)
• Sliced Garlic Chips & Whole Red Chili: 3 cloves + 1 chili
• Large Kachumber Salad: 150g cucumber, tomato, radish
Cooking Steps:
  1. Cook Thick Dal: Pressure cook 70g dal in 2.5 cups water, haldi, salt, and garlic for 3 whistles. Whisk for 30 seconds into a rich, velvety thick stew.
  2. The Mustard Oil Tadka: Heat 5g mustard oil in a small pan. Add cumin seeds, pinch of hing, and sliced garlic chips. Fry until nutty golden brown. Add whole red chili.
  3. Aroma Lock: Turn off heat, add 1/4 tsp degi mirch, pour sizzling into the dal, and cover instantly with lid for 2 minutes to trap the smoky garlic aroma!
  4. Steam 45g basmati rice in 90ml water. Serve steaming dal over rice with big salad dressed in lemon and chaat masala.

Crispy Veggie Chilla & Chilled Peanut Butter Shake

CAL 497
PROTEIN 24.7g
FAT 23.1g
CARBS 48.5g
Ingredients:
• Besan (Bengal Gram Flour, dry): 50g (1 large chilla)
• Natural Peanut Butter: 1 level tbsp (16g)
• Toned Cow's Milk (cold): 250ml (half pouch)
• Diced Onions, Capsicum, Tomato, Herbs: 100g
• Cold-Pressed Oil (for tawa): 4g
Cooking Steps:
  1. Make Chilla: Whisk 50g besan with water, palm-rubbed ajwain, turmeric, cumin, salt, and all diced vegetables. Pour on hot tawa with 4g oil, cook until golden and crisp on both sides.
  2. Make PB Shake: Add 250ml cold milk, 1 level tbsp (16g) peanut butter, and a generous pinch of cinnamon into a shaker bottle. Shake 20 seconds into a frothy, thick shake!
  3. Eat the hot crunchy chilla with the cold peanut butter shake. Best pre-workout fuel: sustained energy without sluggishness!

Soya Curry, Phulka & Warm Haldi Milk with Almonds

CAL 518
PROTEIN 36.3g
FAT 16.6g
CARBS 56.5g
Ingredients:
• Soya Chunks (dry raw): 40g (~120g curried)
• Whole Wheat / Jowar Atta: 35g (1 roti)
• Toned Milk (warm): 250ml (remaining half of pouch)
• Soaked & Peeled Almonds: 8g (~5-6 nuts)
• Cold-Pressed Oil: 4g
Cooking Steps:
  1. Boil 40g soya chunks 5 mins, drain, shock in cold water, and squeeze dry.
  2. Heat 4g oil. Sauté bay leaf, cardamom, clove, and onions until deep golden. Add tomato puree, ginger-garlic, haldi, chili, and coriander. Sauté soya chunks 3 mins, add 150ml water, simmer 8 mins. Finish with kasuri methi.
  3. Cook 1 puffed roti from 35g flour.
  4. 8:00 PM Golden Milk: Heat 250ml milk with haldi, crushed cardamom, cinnamon, and black pepper. Drink warm with your 5-6 peeled soaked almonds. Your window is officially closed at 8:00 PM!
